About Me
My name is Judith Barrios. I'm a student at Houston City College studying AI and its applications in the real world. I took this course because I wanted to understand how AI actually works outside of a laptop — on real devices, in real networks, in real industrial settings.

Before this class I mostly thought about AI in terms of training models. This course pushed me to think about what happens after that — how do you get a model onto a small device? How do sensors talk to each other? What happens when a network gets congested? Those questions are what this portfolio is about.

Things I Took Away From This Course
Deploying a model is a completely different challenge from training one
The reward function in reinforcement learning matters just as much as the algorithm
A network can look fine by traditional metrics and still be giving the controller stale data
Every protocol in Lab 04 broke in a different way, and that was actually the most useful part
Planning for what goes wrong matters more than picking the right tools
